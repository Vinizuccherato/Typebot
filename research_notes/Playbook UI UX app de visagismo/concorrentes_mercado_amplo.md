# Wider competitor landscape for an iOS visagism / face-analysis app ("facet.") — beyond Qoves, Cephra, FaceIQ

> Research date: 2026-11-17. Method note: these notes rest mainly on web-search result snippets. Full-page fetches were blocked in this session for screensdesign.com, aol.com, realworldappeal.com and ugcmaxx.com, so teardown details are taken from search-indexed titles and summaries rather than pages read in full. Many sources are App Store listings, affiliate "best apps 2026" pages or competitor blogs, so they are marketing or biased. Each claim below says what kind of source backs it.

## 1. Which apps lead each sub-category?

### Takeaway
The category is crowded and long-tail. There are dozens of near-identical "Face Shape Finder" and "LooksMax AI" clones on the App Store, often sharing a name. A few names stand out: **Umax** (looksmaxxing), **LooksMax AI**, **Mogger** and **Mogged** (rating), **Hiface** (face shape), **Warby Parker** and **Zenni** (eyewear try-on), **YouCam / Perfect Corp** (makeup, brows, hair try-on, the only public company with audited numbers), and a growing set of **Brazilian/Portuguese visagism tools** (PandaMi, Visagisme AI, BarberVision) plus **PMU brow-mapping apps** for professionals (Brow Mapping AI / NE Pigments, Brow Designer, TryBrows).

### Inferences
- The consumer face-shape and looksmax segments are commoditised. They are clone-heavy, compete on SEO-style App Store names, and are built on the same commodity landmark models (MediaPipe-type meshes of 68 or 478 points). Name confusion (Umax / U Max / Umax AI / Youmax) shows weak brand moats.
- The defensible players are those with either (1) commerce attached (Warby Parker, Zenni and YouCam monetise products or brands, not the analysis) or (2) a professional workflow (NE Pigments ties brow mapping to its pigment catalogue). "facet." would sit between these. Professional visagism in Portuguese is thin: PandaMi is the only visible incumbent, and it is a small vendor.

### Gaps
- No verified rankings by category (Sensor Tower or Appfigures charts were not reachable). Hiface's install count was not found.
- ModiFace (L'Oréal) app-level details were not found. ModiFace is now mainly a B2B engine inside brand apps and sites, not a standalone consumer app (no source confirmed in this session).
- Jeeliz: not researched. It is a B2B web SDK for glasses try-on, not a consumer app.

## 2. Per-app profiles (value proposition, monetisation, onboarding, capture, results, ratings, revenue)

### Takeaway
The best-documented app is Umax. It runs a roughly 6-step onboarding, then selfie upload, then **blurred results** that unlock only through a **$3.99/week** subscription or by **inviting 3 friends**. Its revenue claims are founder-reported and inconsistent ($65k/month by a third-party estimate up to $500k/month claimed). LooksMax-type clones copy this pattern, and paywall complaints dominate reviews. Try-on apps (Warby Parker, Zenni, YouCam) are free funnels into commerce or subscriptions. Gen-AI hairstyle apps run credit and weekly-subscription models from $3.99 to $9.99/week.

## 3. Common patterns across the category, and gaps / opportunities

### Takeaway
The dominant funnel in looksmaxxing and face-rating apps is: **short onboarding quiz → selfie capture → "analysing" moment → blurred or locked results → weekly-subscription paywall, or "invite 3 friends" as the alternative unlock**, with a **0–10 score plus sub-scores** and a **shareable screenshot** as the viral unit. The main gaps are trust (score instability, ethnic bias, mental-health criticism), professional-grade measurement (mm-scale, calibrated), and actionable, non-judgmental output. 3D and TrueDepth capture is used by eyewear retailers but rarely by analysis apps.

### Cited Findings
- Blurred results after upload, unlocked by Pro or by inviting friends (Umax; LooksMax AI) — [ScreensDesign Umax (snippet)](https://screensdesign.com/showcase/umax-become-hot); [techbriefly](https://techbriefly.com/ai-tools/looksmax-ai/)
- The sequencing complaint: "the catch isn't fraud; it's sequencing: you upload your face first and meet the $3.99/week paywall after" — [realworldappeal (snippet; competitor-affiliated)](https://realworldappeal.com/en/blog/umax-ai)
- Weekly pricing ($3.99–$9.99/week) is standard across looksmax and hair try-on apps — [App Store LooksMax PSL](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id6768808833); [HairStyleShare](https://hairstyleshare.com/); [NewLook](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/newlook-ai-hairstyle-try-on/id6771566747)
- Score instability: the same photo scores differently on rescan (Umax reviews). Forums report that the same face scores wildly differently across apps — [JustUseApp](https://justuseapp.com/en/app/6471026798/umax-maximize-your-looks/reviews); [AOL/Fortune summary](https://www.aol.com/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-163942511.html)
- Shareable score as the growth loop (users post their scores in TikTok videos) — [AOL/Fortune](https://www.aol.com/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-163942511.html); [ugcmaxx](https://ugcmaxx.com/blog/umax-tiktok-strategy/)
- Counter-positioning is emerging. Content sites sell "no paywall", "rating-free" or "free, no subscription" alternatives (Mogged, The Face Report, glowupface "rating apps vs rating-free tools") — [glowupface](https://glowupface.com/articles/best-looksmaxxing-apps); [The Face Report](https://thefacereport.com/guides/umax-alternative); [miora](https://getmiora.com/blog/best-looksmaxxing-apps-2026)
- Ethical criticism: psychologists say looksmaxxing apps rating teen boys worsen the youth mental-health crisis — [AOL/Fortune headline](https://www.aol.com/looksmaxxing-apps-rate-teen-boys-163942511.html). A December 2025 Aesthetic Surgery Journal Open Forum article warns these communities can push appearance-seeking toward self-harm (cited in Brazilian press) — [Brasil em Folhas, May 2026](https://www.brasilemfolhas.com.br/2026/05/looksmaxxing-a-busca-extrema-pela-beleza-entre-jovens-e-seus-riscos/)

- Measurement precision as a differentiator: The Face Report claims about ±1 mm brow offsets via iris-diameter calibration. Manual string mapping can carry 1–2 cm errors (product-listing claim) — [The Face Report](https://thefacereport.com/tools/eyebrow-mapping); [brow mapping search summary / Walmart listing](https://www.walmart.com/ip/Microblading-Mapping-String-Ruler-Brow-Positioning-Tool-PMU-Accessory-Permanent-Makeup-Pre-Inked-Eyebrow-Line-Mark-Ruler/18448865242)
- 3D capture exists in eyewear (Warby Parker TrueDepth, Zenni 180° video scan) — [Warby Parker](https://www.warbyparker.com/app); [Zenni App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zenni-try-on-buy-glasses/id1524212453)
- Hand-off to a professional: PandaMi exports a PDF for the stylist, and Visagisme AI gives "technical instructions for the professional" — [PandaMi](https://pandami.com.br/); [Visagisme AI](https://www.visagismeai.com/)
- On-device processing is used as a privacy claim by free web tools (SkinPal, Calqora) — [Calqora](https://www.calqora.com/face-analysis); [SkinPal](https://skinpalai.app/tools/eyebrow-mapping)

### Inferences
- **Patterns to borrow, adapted ethically:** a short personalisation quiz before capture (goals, hair type, style), a guided multi-angle capture, an "analysing" reveal moment, sub-scores and labels, a shareable result card, a professional-ready PDF export, and "picks + avoid" style recommendations.
- **Patterns to avoid or reframe:** blur-after-upload with no free value (the main source of 1-star reviews), weekly-only pricing, a single attractiveness number aimed at teens, and score volatility. A "facet." could show the face-shape label and two or three key proportions for free, paywall the full visagism plan, try-ons and PDF, and offer annual pricing with a trial. Stable, repeatable measurement (same photo gives the same result) would directly answer a documented pain point.
- **White space:** (1) Portuguese-first professional visagism for salons, barbers and brow artists, where PandaMi is small; (2) calibrated mm measurement and TrueDepth 3D for analysis rather than shopping; (3) inclusive standards (Brazil's mixed-ethnicity population, given the reported ethnic bias in scores); (4) visagism framed as identity and expression ("what fits your features and personality") rather than ranking.

### Inferences
- Brazil is a favourable but sensitive launch market. Demand peaked in early 2026 and TikTok now restricts the "looksmaxxing" term, so organic TikTok acquisition built on looksmax keywords is riskier in 2026 than it was for Umax in 2024. Positioning on "visagismo" (an established, salon-legitimate term in Brazil) avoids both the restriction and the stigma.
- BRL price points (around R$ 4,90 tiers) suggest regional pricing well below US weekly rates. A Brazilian app should localise pricing, and possibly payments, accordingly.
- The professional channel (salons, barbers, PMU artists) is large in Brazil and media are already flagging looksmax clients. A B2B2C model (the professional uses facet. in the chair and the client gets the report) is a plausible differentiator, as NE Pigments does with brow mapping.
